## Definite articles

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | **der** | **die** | **das** | **die** |
| Akkusativ | **den** | **die** | **das** | **die** |
| Dativ | **dem** | **der** | **dem** | **den** |
| Genitiv | **des** | **der** | **des** | **der** |

Note that dative plural nouns add an **-n** unless the plural already ends in **-n** or **-s**.

- **mit den Bücher-n** → but **mit den Frauen** (already ends in -n)

## Indefinite articles

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | **ein** | **eine** | **ein** |
| Akkusativ | **einen** | **eine** | **ein** |
| Dativ | **einem** | **einer** | **einem** |
| Genitiv | **eines** | **einer** | **eines** |

There is no indefinite article in the plural. Use **keine** (or other ein-words) for the plural forms.

## Negation: kein

**kein** declines like **ein** but also has plural forms.

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | **kein** | **keine** | **kein** | **keine** |
| Akkusativ | **keinen** | **keine** | **kein** | **keine** |
| Dativ | **keinem** | **keiner** | **keinem** | **keinen** |
| Genitiv | **keines** | **keiner** | **keines** | **keiner** |

## Demonstratives: dieser, jener

**dieser** (this) and **jener** (that) decline like the definite article with the adjective endings added.

| Case | m | f | n | pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | **dieser** | **diese** | **dieses** | **diese** |
| Akkusativ | **diesen** | **diese** | **dieses** | **diese** |
| Dativ | **diesem** | **dieser** | **diesem** | **diesen** |
| Genitiv | **dieses** | **dieser** | **dieses** | **dieser** |

## Gender clues by noun ending

No rule is 100% reliable, but these patterns are helpful.

### Usually masculine

- **-er** (many): **der Vater, der Lehrer, der Apfel**
- **-ismus, -ist**: **der Kapitalismus, der Polizist**
- **-ling**: **der Frühling, der Lehrling**
- Days, months, seasons: **der Montag, der Januar, der Sommer**
- Most verbs used as nouns: **das Lesen** → but the verbal noun itself is neuter; many other derived nouns with **-er** are masculine

### Usually feminine

- **-e** (very common): **die Blume, die Sprache, die Schule**
- **-ung**: **die Wohnung, die Zeitung**
- **-heit, -keit**: **die Freiheit, die Möglichkeit**
- **-schaft**: **die Freundschaft, die Wissenschaft**
- **-ei**: **die Bäckerei, die Partei**
- **-in** (female): **die Lehrerin, die Ärztin**
- **-ion**: **die Information, die Station**
- **-ik**: **die Musik, die Grammatik**

### Usually neuter

- **-chen, -lein** (diminutives): **das Mädchen, das Häuslein**
- **-ment**: **das Medikament, das Dokument**
- **-tum**: **das Eigentum, das Königtum**
- Geographical names ending in **-um**: **das Museum, das Zentrum**
- Infinitives used as nouns: **das Essen, das Schwimmen**
- Most metals and chemical elements: **das Gold, das Eisen**

### Variable with -e

Many nouns ending in **-e** are feminine, but there are important exceptions, especially masculine ones denoting people or agents.

- **der Name, der Gedanke, der Junge**
- **der Löwe, der Affe**

## Notes

- Compound nouns take the gender of the last element: **die Kühlschranktür** (die Tür).
