## Definite article (weak endings)

Used with **der / das / die** and with **dieser, jener, jeder, mancher, welcher**.

Also used with plural forms such as **diese, jene, manche, solche, welche, alle, keine, meine**.

| Case | m | n | f | pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nom | -e | -e | -e | -en |
| akk | -en | -e | -e | -en |
| dat | -en | -en | -en | -en |
| gen | -en | -en | -en | -en |

## Indefinite article (mixed endings)

Used with singular **ein-** words such as **kein, mein, irgendein**.

Plural is not used for **ein / irgendein**. Plural **keine / meine** goes with the **definite article table** above.

| Case | m | n | f | pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nom | -er | -es | -e | -en |
| akk | -en | -es | -e | -en |
| dat | -en | -en | -en | -en |
| gen | -en | -en | -en | -en |

## No article (strong endings)

Used with **no article**.

Also used after plural words such as **einige, mehrere, viele, wenige, Zahlen (numbers)**.

| Case | m | n | f | pl |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nom | -er | -es | -e | -e |
| akk | -en | -es | -e | -e |
| dat | -em | -em | -er | -en |
| gen | -en | -en | -er | -er |

## Komparativ and Superlativ

### Komparativ

Usually add **-er** to the adjective.

- **schnell → schneller**
- **klein → kleiner**

Many one-syllable adjectives with **a, o, u** often take an umlaut.

- **alt → älter**
- **groß → größer**
- **jung → jünger**

Used for comparisons, often with **als**.

- **Anna ist größer als Tom.**

### Superlativ

Predicate form usually uses **am + adjective + -sten / -esten**.

- **schnell → am schnellsten**
- **freundlich → am freundlichsten**

Before a noun, use the superlative stem **-st- / -est-** plus normal adjective endings.

- **der schnellste Zug**
- **die freundlichste Lehrerin**

Adjectives ending in **-d, -t, -s, -ß, -sch, -z, -x** often use **-esten**.

- **kalt → am kältesten**
- **heiß → am heißesten**

Some common irregular forms:

- **gut → besser → am besten**
- **viel → mehr → am meisten**
- **gern → lieber → am liebsten**
